Built an automation with **Google Apps Script** for **Diaspora Junior Consulting (DJC)**, AEN's consulting initiative, to speed up client needs assessment.

**How it works**
- A prospective client fills out an intake form describing their needs
- On submission, Apps Script generates a styled HTML email summarizing the request
- The email is sent automatically to the consulting manager for review

This removed the manual step of re-reading and reformatting raw form responses, giving the manager a ready-to-read summary as soon as a client submits a request.

## Contribution and outcome

My role was to implement the intake-to-email workflow. The deliverable is a formatted summary sent to the consulting manager after submission, removing a manual reformatting step.

This is an example of connecting an operational need to a small, maintainable automation. No measured time saving or conversion increase is claimed.

## Operational considerations

For continued operation, ownership of the script, authorized recipients, form access and failure handling should remain explicit. Client intake information should not be exposed in public examples.
