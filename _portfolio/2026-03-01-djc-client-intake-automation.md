I built a Google Apps Script workflow for Diaspora Junior Consulting, the consulting initiative of the African Engineers Network.

When a prospective client submits the intake form, the script formats their responses into an HTML email and sends it to the consulting manager. The manager can review the request without manually reformatting the responses.

Maintenance involves managing script ownership, form access, authorized recipients and failed submissions. Client responses are not included in public examples.
