The second part of my elliptic-curve series covers finite fields and operations on curve points. Geometric illustrations over the real numbers help with intuition; cryptographic implementations use discrete arithmetic.

The guide examines field and curve parameters, point addition and scalar multiplication. These formulas are the mathematical background. Handling exceptional cases, validating inputs and limiting side-channel leakage are separate implementation concerns.
